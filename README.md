## 👨‍⚕️ About Me

I'm a physician interested in the intersection of **medicine, technology, and clinical education**.

I build practical digital tools designed around real clinical workflows, with a particular focus on:

- Emergency Medicine
- Resuscitation
- Trauma Care
- Point-of-Care Clinical Tools
- Clinical Calculators
- Medical Education
- Digital Clinical Workflows

My goal is to turn complex clinical information into tools that are **fast, clear, accessible, and practical**.

## 🚑 Featured Projects

### 🩺 Emergency Toolkit

A lightweight, browser-based **Emergency Medicine point-of-care toolkit** designed for rapid clinical reference.

Includes clinical calculators, emergency algorithms, drug references, responsive mobile support, light/dark modes, and offline-friendly deployment.

**JavaScript · HTML · CSS · Emergency Medicine**

[Explore Emergency Toolkit →](https://github.com/ShaheenMedTech/Emergency-Toolkit)

---

### 📘 Emergency Medicine & Resuscitation

A comprehensive rapid-access clinical reference covering:

**Emergency Medicine · Resuscitation · Trauma · Critical Care**

Built around sequential algorithms, diagnostic hallmarks, pharmacotherapy, clinical pearls, pitfalls, and disposition guidance.

[Explore the Clinical Reference →](https://github.com/ShaheenMedTech/Emergency---Medicine---and---Resuscitation)

---

### ⚡ ShiftMate ER

A lightweight application for **Emergency Department shift management and clinical handover**.

Features include cases, tasks, notes, reminders, bilingual Arabic/English support, RTL/LTR layouts, local data storage, and light/dark themes.

**React · Vite · Tailwind CSS · JavaScript**

[Explore ShiftMate ER →](https://github.com/ShaheenMedTech/ShiftMate-ER)
